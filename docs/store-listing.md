## Full description

Run your pig farm's records the way you actually work — one farm, one
team, everything in one place.

**Breeding, done right**
Track estrus cycles, breeding records, and litters with real gestation
math (114 days, not the internet's usual guess), automatic farrowing
windows, and an inbreeding calculator that checks every mating against
your farm's own thresholds before it happens — not after.

**Health & vaccinations**
Log treatments, medications, and withdrawal periods per animal or per
batch. Vaccination protocols run on a schedule, so due dates surface
before they're missed, and outbreak alerts flag unusual case clusters
automatically.

**Feed & growth**
Feeding programs, feed batches, and weight records tie together into
real FCR and growth-rate numbers, not spreadsheets built after the fact.

**Money, in your farm's currency**
Transactions, sales, and supplier records, with every amount handled
precisely (no rounding drift) and reports built for the person doing
the books, not a generic accounting template.

**Built for a team, not just an owner**
Invite staff with the right role — Manager, Veterinarian, or Worker —
each seeing exactly what their job needs. An audit trail keeps a
record of who changed what.

**Your farm's data stays your farm's**
Every farm's records are isolated from every other farm's, enforced at
the database level, not just hidden in the interface.
